CHAPTER ONE · OUR FIRST DATE

Every Love Has a Story

And ours is one of my favourite things to remember.

THE BEGINNING

On the 28th of November 2025

We went on our very first date at Seattle Coffee Co. We were both nervous, each trying to navigate the excitement and uncertainty of meeting in a different way. I spent much of the time laughing, looking around, and capturing little moments on my phone, while you occasionally glanced at yours, quietly searching for the perfect conversation starters to break the silence.

As Time Went By

Our initial nervousness gradually faded. We became more comfortable with each other, and our conversations began to flow naturally. At some point, the barista revealed that I had introduced you as my friend. She even mentioned to me that it was quite obvious you liked me, judging by how nervous you had been.

Eventually

Our date came to an end, and I drove you to meet up with your friends. But before you stepped out of the car, we both took a moment to express just how wonderful the date had been. Then, almost instinctively, we leaned towards each other, and our lips met for the very first time. It was a magical moment, one neither of us seemed ready to let go of. The kiss was so special that we found ourselves going back for more, each time playfully saying, "One more."

Later that evening

During our phone call, neither of us could stop talking about the date. The excitement of the day was still fresh, and it was clear that the moments we had shared meant something special to both of us.

Finally

And then came the question that would make our day even more unforgettable. You officially asked me, using these exact words:

"WOULD YOU BE MINE?"

To be continued, forever. ✧
